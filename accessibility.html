Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Our commitment

CIRIS Limited wants everyone to be able to use this website, whatever their ability or the technology they use. This statement covers the website at cirisgroup.co.uk.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 at level AA. This is the standard the UK government expects of public sector websites, and we hold ourselves to it because many of our clients do.

What you can do on this website

We have designed this website so that you should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen or needing to scroll sideways

  • use your browser settings to change text size, colours and fonts

  • navigate the whole site using just a keyboard, with a clear highlight showing where you are

  • use a screen reader, with headings, links and images properly labelled

  • read text with strong colour contrast against its background

  • view the site on a phone, tablet or desktop

  • browse without movement or animation if your device is set to reduce motion

We have used plain English throughout. AbilityNet has advice on making your device easier to use if you have a disability.

Known limitations

We are not aware of any parts of this website that fall short of WCAG 2.2 AA.

Some documents we link to, such as PDFs from other organisations, may not be fully accessible. We cannot control those, but we will help you get the information another way.

Feedback, alternative formats and contact

If you find a problem with this website, or need information in a different format such as large print, easy read or a Word document, please contact us:

  • Email: info@cirisgroup.co.uk

  • Phone: +44 7889 179648

We will reply within 5 working days. If we cannot fix a problem straight away, we will tell you when we expect to and help you get what you need in the meantime.

How we tested this website

We tested this website in November 2026 using:

  • automated checks with WAVE and the Lighthouse accessibility audit built into Chrome

  • manual checks of keyboard navigation, zoom to 400% and colour contrast

  • spot checks with a screen reader (NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on iPhone)

We test again whenever we make significant changes to the site.

Review of this statement

This statement was prepared on 5th November 2026. We review it at least once a year.
