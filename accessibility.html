Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Draft for director review before launch.

CIRIS wants everyone to be able to use this website. We have designed it to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

Text and buttons meet colour-contrast standards. The site can be used with a keyboard and screen reader, works on phones and when zoomed to 200%, and respects your device’s “reduce motion” setting by turning off animations.

Feedback

If you find anything difficult to use, or need information in a different format, please email info@cirisgroup.co.uk and we will respond as soon as we can.
