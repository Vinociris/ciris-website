How we work

Four stages, with you at the centre.

Every CIRIS engagement follows our 4D model. Each stage is grounded in rigorous analysis, so our support rests on a real understanding of your challenge, is shaped with you, carried through to delivery and used to build lasting capability.

  1. Discover

    We analyse your challenge and its context to build a clear, shared understanding.

    We start by understanding you, your needs and the challenge you face. Our analysts examine the information you already hold, identify what is missing and assess the wider context and how the picture may change in the future.

    Everything that follows rests on a clear, evidence-based and shared understanding.

  2. Design

    We co-design options with you, each one tested through rigorous analysis.

    We work through potential solutions with you, using analysis to test each option against the evidence, the risks and the outcomes you need. We are candid about the strengths and weaknesses of every approach.

    Design concludes with a clear pathway and a solid plan to help you achieve your outcomes.

  3. Deliver

    We stay alongside you to turn plans into results.

    Too many plans remain on the shelf or fail to realise their intended benefits. We stay alongside you through implementation, providing practical support to turn plans into results and realise the benefits you intended.

  4. Develop

    We analyse what was achieved and build your own capability for the future.

    Development closes the loop and informs what comes next. Through structured debriefs and evaluation, we analyse the benefits achieved against the outcomes intended and learn from every engagement.

    We then pass that knowledge on through training, workshops and mentoring in analysis, critical thinking, intelligence management and risk management, helping you become more self-sufficient and better prepared for future challenges.
