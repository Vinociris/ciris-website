Services

Information into insight. Insight into understanding. Understanding into action.

CIRIS combines collective intelligence, risk expertise and practical insight to help organisations anticipate threats, understand complexity, make informed decisions and build safer, more resilient futures. Our work is practical and deliverable, not theoretical reports.

CIRIS Intelligence

Intelligence

Turning information into understanding that supports action. Being truly intelligence-led is smart business.

  • Intelligence frameworks and operating models
  • Intelligence maturity reviews
  • Strategic assessments
  • Intelligence-led problem solving

CIRIS Risk

Risk

Very few decisions, including doing nothing, come without risk. We help you identify, clarify and manage it so objectives are achieved.

  • Strategic and operational risk assessment
  • Risk modelling and prioritisation
  • Decision-support frameworks
  • Governance and assurance reviews

CIRIS Insight

Insight

Data only adds value when it changes decisions. We help you see the bigger picture and turn evidence into clear, prioritised actions.

  • Strategic and tactical analysis
  • Threat and vulnerability assessments
  • Research and evidence synthesis
  • Data-informed decision support
  • AI-enhanced analytical development

CIRIS Change

Change

Helping you anticipate and prepare for change, whether that is reform, transformation or a threat that has not yet arrived.

  • Horizon scanning and foresight
  • Emerging threat and risk analysis
  • Scenario planning and exercises
  • Organisational resilience and transformation
  • Future capability development

CIRIS Training

Training

We pass on what we know, so your people become more self-sufficient and better prepared for future challenges.

  • Critical thinking training
  • Intelligence management training
  • Risk management training
  • Workshops and mentoring
  • Professional development programmes

Who we work with

Organisations going through significant change, from reform in policing and border security to local government transformation, and any organisation that needs to understand risk and act on it.

  • Police forces and law enforcement agencies
  • Government departments and public bodies
  • Local authorities and community safety partnerships
  • Regulators and oversight bodies
  • Critical infrastructure and corporate security
  • International policing, anti-corruption and public safety
  • Private sector organisations
  • Academia
  • Charities and the third sector
