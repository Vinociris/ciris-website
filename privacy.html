Privacy notice

CIRIS Limited (company number 17452319, registered office 9 King Street, Much Wenlock, TF13 6BL) is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. Contact us about privacy at info@cirisgroup.co.uk.

What we collect and why

If you use our enquiry form or email us, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email address, and optionally your organisation, phone number and message. We use them only to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver our services. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in responding to you, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who we share it with

Enquiry form messages are passed to our inbox by Web3Forms, our form-handling provider. Our email is provided by Microsoft 365. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to [two years] unless you become a client, in which case we keep records for as long as needed for the contract and our legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking analytics. Our fonts and images are served from our own website.
