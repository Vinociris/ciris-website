Privacy

Privacy notice

1. Who we are and how to contact us

CIRIS Limited is a consultancy registered in England and Wales, company number 17452319. Our registered office is 9 King Street, Much Wenlock, TF13 6BL.

We are the controller for the personal information described in this notice. This means we decide how and why it is used. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration number ZC280912.

This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, and what rights you have. It covers our website, enquiries, our work with clients and associates, and our business contacts.

If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your information, contact our data protection lead, Colin Mattinson:

  • Email: info@cirisgroup.co.uk

  • Post: Data Protection Lead, CIRIS Limited, 9 King Street, Much Wenlock, TF13 6BL

2. Personal information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you, deliver our work and run our business.

If you are… We may hold Where it comes from
A website visitor IP address, browser and device type, pages visited Automatically, through our website hosting and security providers (StackCP / 20i and Cloudflare)
Someone who contacts us Name, job title, organisation, email, phone number, and what you tell us You
A client contact Name, job title, organisation, contact details, meeting notes and correspondence You or your organisation
An associate, supplier or partner Name, contact details, business and payment details, qualifications and vetting information where a contract requires it You, or vetting bodies with your knowledge
A business contact Name, job title, organisation and contact details You, events, or professional networks such as LinkedIn

We do not set out to collect special category information about you (such as health or ethnicity) for our own purposes. Information we handle on behalf of clients is covered in section 4.

3. How we use it and our lawful bases

UK data protection law requires a lawful basis for each use of your information. Ours are set out below.

What we use it for Lawful basis
Replying to enquiries and discussing possible work Legitimate interests: responding to people who contact us
Agreeing and delivering contracts with clients, associates and suppliers Contract, or steps before entering one
Invoicing, accounts, tax and company records Legal obligation
Vetting associates where a client contract requires it Legal obligation or legitimate interests, with your knowledge
Keeping our website secure and working Legitimate interests: protecting our systems
Sending occasional updates about our services to business contacts Legitimate interests; you can opt out at any time
Responding to legal claims Legitimate interests or legal obligation

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed our interests against yours. You can object at any time (see section 10).

We do not sell your information. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Client work where we act on a client's behalf

Much of our work involves reviewing information held by our clients, such as police forces, government bodies and other organisations. That information may include sensitive personal data, including information about victims, offenders and vulnerable people.

In that work, our client is usually the controller, and we act as their processor. We use the information only on their written instructions and under a contract that sets out how it must be protected. For law enforcement clients, the processing may fall under Part 3 of the Data Protection Act 2018.

If your information is part of a client's records, the client's own privacy notice explains how it is used. Please contact that organisation to exercise your rights. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to them promptly.

Wherever possible, we work with anonymised data.

5. Who we share it with

We share your information only where we need to, and only with organisations bound to protect it.

  • IT and hosting providers: Microsoft (email and document storage), our managed IT provider (Fresh Tech), Cloudflare (website security and delivery), GitHub (website code), StackCP / 20i (website hosting) and Web3Forms (which passes messages sent through our website’s enquiry form to our inbox)

  • Professional advisers: our accountants, lawyers and insurers

  • Associates: consultants working with us on a specific engagement, under confidentiality terms

  • Clients: where needed to deliver work you are involved in

  • Authorities: HMRC, regulators, courts or the police where the law requires it

If CIRIS is restructured or sold, your information may pass to the new owner under the same protections.

6. International transfers

We aim to keep your information in the UK or the European Economic Area. Some of our providers, such as Microsoft, Cloudflare and GitHub, may process data in other countries, including the United States.

Where our main providers store data:

Microsoft 365: our Microsoft 365 tenant is provisioned in the UK, so Microsoft commits to storing core customer data, such as email and documents, in the UK. Some processing may take place overseas. Microsoft explains these arrangements and its UK GDPR protections in its Microsoft 365 data storage commitments, Microsoft privacy and data protection information, and the Microsoft Data Protection Addendum.

Cloudflare: mainly the United States and the European Economic Area, with processing and access worldwide depending on the service and localisation settings. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

GitHub: standard GitHub cloud services store data in the United States, and personal data may also be processed in other countries. See GitHub's privacy statement.

StackCP / 20i: the United Kingdom. See 20i's privacy policy.

Web3Forms: passes enquiry form messages to our Microsoft 365 inbox and may process them outside the UK. We use it only to receive enquiries, not to store them. See Web3Forms' privacy policy.

We use Cloudflare, GitHub, StackCP / 20i and Web3Forms only to run our website. We do not use them to store client information or other sensitive personal data, and all of these accounts are protected by multi-factor authentication.

Where data is transferred outside the UK, we make sure the transfer is protected by UK-approved safeguards. These include transfers to countries the UK Government has approved, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (the UK-US data bridge). You can ask us for details.

7. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it, then delete it securely.

Information How long we keep it
Enquiries that don't lead to work 2 years from last contact
Client, associate and supplier contracts and correspondence 6 years after the contract ends
Financial and tax records 6 years after the end of the financial year
Business contact details for updates Until you opt out, or 3 years without contact
Website server logs Up to 30 days, unless needed to investigate a security issue
Information we process for clients As the client's contract instructs; returned or deleted at the end of the engagement

8. How we keep it secure

We protect your information with measures suited to the sensitivity of the work we do. These include:

  • encrypted email and storage, with multi-factor authentication on all accounts

  • access limited to the people who need it for a specific task

  • managed, updated devices, with Cyber Essentials certification

  • confidentiality terms with everyone who works with us

  • handling client information to the standard the client's contract requires, including any vetting or government security classification requirements

If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

9. Cookies and website analytics

We do not use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking. Cloudflare, which protects our website, may set strictly necessary security cookies to tell genuine visitors apart from automated attacks. These cookies do not identify you, and the law does not require consent for them.

If we add any non-essential cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access a copy of the information we hold about you

  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete

  • erase your information in some circumstances

  • restrict how we use your information while a concern is resolved

  • object to our use of your information based on legitimate interests, and to stop marketing at any time

  • move information you gave us to another organisation, where we rely on contract or consent

To use any of these rights, email info@cirisgroup.co.uk. There is no charge in most cases. We may need to confirm your identity. We will reply within one month, or, for complex requests, within up to two further months, in which case we will tell you within the first month.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first at info@cirisgroup.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, keep you updated on progress, and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office:

  • Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint

  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113

  • Post: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

12. Changes to this notice

We review this notice at least once a year and whenever our services or the law change. The latest version will always be on this page.
